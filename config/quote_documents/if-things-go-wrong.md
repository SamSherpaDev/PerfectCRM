What our booking terms say about delays, small groups and changed plans.

## If weather delays your Lukla flight

If weather delays your Lukla flight and you need to wait, we cover up to 2 extra nights while you wait, and more when the situation calls for it. Nights you choose to add, or a helicopter you choose instead of waiting, are your cost, quoted before anything is booked.

## If only two travelers book

Scheduled departures have no minimum group size. Your scheduled date can run with two travelers; we do not cancel because too few travelers book. Unsafe conditions or government restrictions can still prevent a trip from running.

## If an advisory changes or your trek region closes

If the US State Department raises Nepal to Level 3 or higher, or your trek region is closed, within 60 days of departure, you can move your dates free or take full credit for 24 months toward any SherpaHolidays trip. This replaces normal traveler-cancellation charges, including supplier cancellation exceptions. Full credit covers all payments for the affected trip, not just the deposit. A date move has no change fee; we explain any replacement-trip price difference before you decide. This does not replace a cash refund owed if we cancel or cannot provide contracted services.

## If you cancel at least 90 days before departure

Email info@sherpaholidays.com. If we receive your cancellation at least 90 days before your trip starts, your scheduled or private deposit becomes a dollar credit toward any SherpaHolidays trip for 24 months. We confirm the amount and dates in writing. Book within that period, subject to availability. Your new trip is priced when you rebook; you pay any difference. Unused credit keeps its original expiry date. This credit is not a cash refund. Any longer validity or cash-redemption right required by law still applies. Payments above the deposit are refunded, subject only to a specific supplier exception disclosed and accepted before payment.

These are summaries of booking terms SH-TC-2026-10-04, sections 3-7. Read them alongside your full booking terms and any trip-specific differences accepted before payment. They do not change your terms or refunds required by law.
