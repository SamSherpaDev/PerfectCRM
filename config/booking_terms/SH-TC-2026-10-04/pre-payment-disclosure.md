## If you cancel

Send written notice to info@sherpaholidays.com. Cancellation takes effect when we receive it. Days are counted from receipt in Pacific Time to the confirmed trip start date.

| Days before the trip starts | Cancellation outcome |
|---|---|
| 90 or more | Deposit becomes credit toward any SherpaHolidays trip for 24 months. Payments above the deposit are refunded, subject only to a specific supplier exception disclosed and accepted before payment. |
| 89 to 60 | Cancellation charge: 50% of the total trip price |
| 59 to 46 | Cancellation charge: 75% of the total trip price |
| 45 or fewer | Cancellation charge: 100% of the total trip price. No refund. |

Percentages apply to the total confirmed trip price, including booked supplements and additions. Payments above the applicable charge are refunded. No supplier charge is counted twice. Hotel exceptions must be stated above before payment; without a stated exception, the standard table applies. Exceptions cannot remove the early-cancellation deposit credit, the full conditions-promise credit or mandatory rights.

The deposit credit may be applied to a new booking made within 24 months of issue, subject to availability and the new trip's price. Any unused credit keeps its original expiration date. Longer validity or cash-redemption rights required by law still apply.

**Conditions promise:** If the US State Department raises Nepal to Level 3 or higher, or your trek region is closed, within 60 days of departure, you can move your dates free or take full credit toward any SherpaHolidays trip for 24 months. This replaces the normal traveler-cancellation charges for a qualifying event, including the 45-day rule. We disclose any replacement-trip price difference before you decide.

Comprehensive travel insurance is required, including emergency medical treatment, emergency evacuation, trip cancellation and interruption, and repatriation.
