## PAYMENT

Prices are in US dollars unless stated otherwise. Scheduled prices are per person. Your quote states room occupancy, any single-room supplement, inclusions, exclusions and all mandatory amounts payable for your journey. Private sample itineraries and from prices are not confirmed reservations; we agree the final plan and price before booking. International flights are excluded unless your written itinerary expressly includes them.

The deposit is part of the total price, not an additional fee. A scheduled booking made more than 90 days before departure requires $500 per person. A private journey booked more than 90 days before departure requires 30% of the trip price. The balance is due 90 days before departure. At 90 days or fewer, full payment is due at booking. Your disclosure and invoice state the exact amounts and dates.

Once your deposit is received, your price is locked except for government taxes, park fees or levies introduced after that date. We tell you in writing as soon as we learn of one and identify its amount and basis. A different route, hotel upgrade or optional activity is not an automatic extra charge; any additional cost requires your agreement before it is booked.

We do not charge a stored payment method without your authorization for the amount and payment date. If a balance is overdue, we contact you before treating the booking as canceled for nonpayment. Any cancellation charge must follow the terms you accepted. A failed charge alone does not authorize a different fee or a later, harsher cancellation tier.

## CREDIT

For a traveler cancellation received 90 or more days before departure, the scheduled or private deposit stays as a dollar credit toward any SherpaHolidays trip for 24 months. We confirm the amount, issue date and expiration date in writing. You may apply it to a new booking made within that period, subject to availability. The new trip is priced when you rebook; you pay any difference. Unused credit remains available until its original expiration date. It is not a cash refund. Any longer validity or cash-redemption right required by law still applies.

## CONDITIONS

If the US State Department raises Nepal to Level 3 or higher, or your trek region is closed, within 60 days of departure, you can move your dates free or take full credit toward any SherpaHolidays trip for 24 months. This applies instead of the normal traveler-cancellation charges, including the 45-day rule and any supplier cancellation exception.

Full credit means the payments you made for the affected trip, not only the deposit. We record the trigger and your choice in writing. The credit follows Section 4's use and validity terms. A free date move has no change fee; we disclose any price difference for the replacement trip before you decide. The promise does not replace a cash refund owed when we cancel or cannot provide contracted services.

## SELLER

Scheduled departures have no minimum group size. We do not cancel them because too few travelers book. This is not a promise to operate through unsafe conditions or government restrictions.

Weather, natural disasters, government action, border or route closures, civil unrest, epidemics and other events beyond our reasonable control may prevent a trip or service from operating. We notify you promptly if we cancel or cannot provide contracted services. We refund payments for services not provided as required by California law. We do not make an automatic deduction for unrecoverable supplier costs or force you to accept credit. You may choose an alternative trip or credit in writing after we explain the options.

Where California Business and Professions Code Section 17550.14 requires a refund, we return the required amount within the applicable deadline: the earlier of 30 days from the relevant scheduled departure, refund request or seller cancellation, or three days after we first cannot provide the services. We do not wait for a supplier to reimburse us before meeting our own refund obligations.

International flights and other arrangements you purchase separately are not part of our refund calculation. We do not promise reimbursement for separately purchased travel or preparation expenses, but nothing here excludes liability or rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## CHANGES

We may change the order of activities, timing or route for weather, access or safety. If a named hotel is unavailable, we provide an alternative of equivalent character and standard. A minor change that preserves the agreed character and standard is not itself a cancellation. We tell you promptly about a material change, explain available alternatives and your applicable refund rights, and obtain agreement before charging more. Calling a change operational or safety-related does not remove a refund required by law.

Mountain flights depend on weather and airline decisions. Lukla flights may use Ramechhap instead of Kathmandu; your itinerary identifies the included transfers. If weather delays your Lukla flight and you need to wait, SherpaHolidays pays for the extra nights. Nights you choose to add, or a helicopter you choose instead of waiting, are your cost, quoted before anything is booked. Other delay arrangements and costs are stated in your written itinerary. We suggest booking your flight home one or two days after the trip ends. That buffer does not guarantee a connection.

We cannot guarantee a summit, pass crossing, mountain view, wildlife sighting or on-time flight.

## ALTITUDE

High-altitude travel can cause serious illness, even in fit travelers. Acclimatization reduces risk but cannot eliminate it. Tell your guide promptly about symptoms. Your guide may stop an ascent, change the route or arrange descent. Rescue and medical treatment depend on location, weather, access and available resources and cannot be guaranteed.

## GENERAL-RISK

Road travel, rafting, wildlife activities and travel in remote places carry risks of illness, injury and death. Experienced staff, briefings and equipment reduce risk but cannot eliminate it. Your itinerary identifies your guide team, including Sherpa guides on Himalayan routes, and the support and equipment included.

## INSURANCE

Comprehensive travel insurance is a condition of every booking. Your policy must cover emergency medical treatment, emergency evacuation, trip cancellation and interruption, and repatriation. Confirm with your insurer that it covers your itinerary's activities and maximum altitude, including technical climbing, rafting, helicopter evacuation and search and rescue where applicable. Provide proof before departure. Consider obtaining cover when you book because some benefits have purchase deadlines.

We are not your insurer. Receiving your documents is not a determination of coverage and does not guarantee payment of a claim. Medical and evacuation providers may require payment in advance. Keep insurer emergency details available during your trip.
