## 3. If you cancel

Email your cancellation to info@sherpaholidays.com. It takes effect when we receive your written notice, not when we reply. We count days by subtracting the date we receive your notice, measured in Pacific Time, from the confirmed trip start date. The trip starts on the first day of your contracted itinerary, not when you leave home or begin the trekking section.

The table above applies to scheduled and private journeys. Percentages are calculated on the total confirmed trip price, including booked supplements and additions, not just on the unpaid balance. At 90 or more days, the deposit portion of a fully paid booking is still eligible for the deposit credit. Payments above the applicable cancellation charge are refunded to the original payment method. A cancellation charge does not exceed the total trip price.

At 45 days or fewer, including the departure date, there is no refund for a voluntary traveler cancellation. The same rule applies to a no-show, late arrival or a voluntary early departure. Illness, work changes and personal circumstances do not automatically change the schedule. Insurance may cover an eligible loss, but we cannot promise that it will.

Some boutique or heritage hotels require nonrefundable payments when reserved. If an exception applies, your pre-payment documents identify the supplier, exact amount and how it changes the standard refund calculation. We do not count the same amount twice or add a supplier charge on top of a percentage charge unless that difference was expressly disclosed and accepted before payment. Supplier exceptions cannot remove the approved early-cancellation deposit credit, our full conditions-promise credit or rights required by law. Without a stated exception, the standard table applies.

Requests to change dates are not guaranteed. We explain availability and any quoted cost before you decide. A date change cannot silently replace your accepted terms or convert a cancellation into an undisclosed charge.

We process traveler-cancellation refunds promptly, normally within 14 business days after receiving notice. A shorter legal deadline takes priority. Your bank may take additional time to post the refund.

## 4. Your 24-month credit

For a traveler cancellation received 90 or more days before departure, the scheduled or private deposit stays as a dollar credit toward any SherpaHolidays trip for 24 months. We confirm the amount, issue date and expiration date in writing. You may apply it to a new booking made within that period, subject to availability. The new trip is priced when you rebook; you pay any difference. Unused credit remains available until its original expiration date. It is not a cash refund. Any longer validity or cash-redemption right required by law still applies.

## 5. Our conditions promise

If the US State Department raises Nepal to Level 3 or higher, or your trek region is closed, within 60 days of departure, you can move your dates free or take full credit toward any SherpaHolidays trip for 24 months. This applies instead of the normal traveler-cancellation charges, including the 45-day rule and any supplier cancellation exception.

Full credit means the payments you made for the affected trip, not only the deposit. We record the trigger and your choice in writing. The credit follows Section 4's use and validity terms. A free date move has no change fee; we disclose any price difference for the replacement trip before you decide. The promise does not replace a cash refund owed when we cancel or cannot provide contracted services.

Weather, natural disasters, government action, border or route closures, civil unrest, epidemics and other events beyond our reasonable control may prevent a trip or service from operating. International flights and other arrangements you purchase separately are not part of our refund calculation. We do not promise reimbursement for separately purchased travel or preparation expenses, but nothing here excludes liability or rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 7. Changes, flights and extra nights

We may change the order of activities, timing or route for weather, access or safety. If a named hotel is unavailable, we provide an alternative of equivalent character and standard. A minor change that preserves the agreed character and standard is not itself a cancellation. We tell you promptly about a material change, explain available alternatives and your applicable refund rights, and obtain agreement before charging more. Calling a change operational or safety-related does not remove a refund required by law.

Mountain flights depend on weather and airline decisions. Lukla flights may use Ramechhap instead of Kathmandu; your itinerary identifies the included transfers. If weather delays your Lukla flight and you need to wait, SherpaHolidays pays for the extra nights. Nights you choose to add, or a helicopter you choose instead of waiting, are your cost, quoted before anything is booked. Other delay arrangements and costs are stated in your written itinerary. We suggest booking your flight home one or two days after the trip ends. That buffer does not guarantee a connection.

We cannot guarantee a summit, pass crossing, mountain view, wildlife sighting or on-time flight.

## 8. Health, fitness and participation

Road travel, rafting, wildlife activities and travel in remote places carry risks of illness, injury and death. Experienced staff, briefings and equipment reduce risk but cannot eliminate it. Your itinerary identifies your guide team, including Sherpa guides on Himalayan routes, and the support and equipment included.

High-altitude travel can cause serious illness, even in fit travelers. Acclimatization reduces risk but cannot eliminate it. Tell your guide promptly about symptoms. Your guide may stop an ascent, change the route or arrange descent. Rescue and medical treatment depend on location, weather, access and available resources and cannot be guaranteed.

Read your trip's walking demands, highest daytime and sleeping altitudes, terrain, technical requirements and gear list before booking. Seek advice from a clinician familiar with your health and altitude travel. Tell us about relevant medical conditions, prior altitude problems, allergies, mobility needs and requested accommodations so we can discuss the trip with you. A room or assistance request is not confirmed until we confirm the arrangement in writing.

Follow reasonable guide instructions, local laws and activity briefings. Guides have final authority on immediate safety decisions. We may stop participation where continued activity creates a serious safety risk, or remove a traveler for dangerous, unlawful or seriously disruptive conduct. Decisions are based on the actual situation and reasonable accommodation where applicable, not a blanket age or diagnosis rule. We explain the reason when practicable. Unused services following a voluntary departure or removal for misconduct are not refunded under the traveler-cancellation schedule; legally required refunds remain available. Additional personal return, treatment and evacuation costs are your responsibility except where law or our own responsibility requires otherwise.
